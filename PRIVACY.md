# Nepal Canvas - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Nepal Canvas (facebook.com/nepalcanvasofficial) is a Nepali news page. The "Nepal Canvas Publisher" app is used only by the page owner to publish the page's own videos to the Nepal Canvas Facebook Page.

## What data we collect
- The app does not collect, store or sell any personal data of Facebook users or page visitors.
- The app only uses the Page's own access token to upload the Page's own videos and to read the Page's own post statistics (views, likes and comment counts).

## What we do not do
- We do not log in other users, read their messages, or access their profiles.
- We do not share any data with third parties.

## Data deletion
The app keeps no user data. If you believe any of your data is held, email nepalcanvas22@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days.
